# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Custom Dock Text does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

## What it stores

The text and icon you choose are saved in a small file in the app's own data folder on your PC, so they come back after a restart. Uninstalling the app deletes that folder.

## What it does not do

- It makes no network requests. The package doesn't declare internet access.
- It doesn't read any other files or apps.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, ads or third-party services.
